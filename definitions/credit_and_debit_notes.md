# Definition: Debit Note vs Credit Note

> **Status:** Draft v1. Readable in-tool.
> **Why it matters here:** When the gap is a **supplier short-ship/over-bill**, the receiver's tool option is to **raise a debit note**; the supplier responds with a **credit note**. External content paraphrased for compliance; sources linked.

---

## Debit note (issued by the BUYER)
A **debit note** is a commercial document the **buyer issues to the supplier** to signal that an amount will be debited from the supplier's account — used to seek a **return or price adjustment**, including for **shortages**, returned goods, or overcharges. [rupeezy — debit vs credit note](https://rupeezy.in/blog/difference-between-debit-note-and-credit-note) [enkash — debit vs credit note](https://www.enkash.com/resources/blog/difference-between-debit-note-credit-note)

It **increases the supplier's obligation to the buyer / decreases what the buyer must pay**. [halsimplify — debit vs credit note](https://www.halsimplify.com/knowledge-center/debit-note-vs-credit-note-difference) In short: **a debit note is issued by the buyer; a credit note is issued by the seller.** [smfgindiacredit](https://www.smfgindiacredit.com/knowledge-center/difference-between-debit-note-and-credit-note.aspx)

**Receiver use:** for the 100 undelivered pieces (if the cause is a supplier short-ship), the receiver raises a **debit note** claiming credit for the shortfall.

---

## Credit note / credit memo (issued by the SUPPLIER)
A **credit note** (credit memo) is issued by the **supplier to the buyer to reduce the amount owed** — effectively a **negative invoice** that decreases the buyer's accounts-payable balance. [linkedin — credit/debit notes guide](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/credit-notes-debit-complete-guide-accounts-payable-mangesh-kondhalkar-cjvtf)

By issuing a debit note the buyer seeks the adjustment; by issuing a credit note the seller acknowledges and provides it. [enkash](https://www.enkash.com/resources/blog/difference-between-debit-note-credit-note) General rule: a **debit note increases** the amount payable, a **credit note reduces** it. [nomi — debit vs credit note](https://www.nomi.co.uk/blog/the-difference-between-a-debit-note-and-a-credit-note/)

---

## Quick reference
| | Debit note | Credit note |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| **Issued by** | Buyer (receiver) | Seller (supplier) |
| **Effect** | ↑ amount supplier owes buyer / ↓ what buyer pays | ↓ amount buyer owes supplier |
| **Typical trigger** | Shortage, return, overcharge claim | Acknowledging the claim; price/qty correction |
| **In our gap** | Receiver claims the 100 short units | Supplier confirms & credits them |

## How the tool uses this
- Gap resolution option **#2** (supplier short-ship): generate/record a **debit note** referencing the PO/invoice line and the missing qty.
- Track the expected **credit note** back from the supplier; leave the gap open (accrued) until it arrives — see `true_up_and_accruals.md`.
